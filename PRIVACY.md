# Privacy

Girlfriend Automate has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

- **Stored on your phone only:** settings, contacts list, API keys and reply history (app-private storage, backups disabled).
- **Sent to your AI provider:** when a message from a contact you chose arrives, the last few messages of that conversation and your persona text are sent to Groq or Google Gemini (whichever you selected) to write a reply. Their privacy terms apply.
- **Permissions:** READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS (to read incoming and past messages), SEND_SMS (to reply), READ_CONTACTS (optional, to match names), INTERNET, POST_NOTIFICATIONS (drafts and urgent alerts).
- Replies always carry an AI label. Do not use this app to deceive anyone.

To erase everything, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
