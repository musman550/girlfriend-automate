Girlfriend Automate

The open-source AI SMS auto-reply assistant for Android. Busy at work, studying or driving? Your AI keeps the chat warm, and always says it is an AI.

Why people use it

Honest by design
Every reply starts with your own AI intro. The AI label cannot be switched off.
Sounds like you
Reads recent chat history and matches your tone in Roman Urdu, Hinglish, English and more.
You stay in control
Approve mode, urgent words, stop words, pause and remove controls.
Private and free
No servers, no ads, no tracking. MIT licensed.

How it works

  1. Install the APK and allow SMS permissions.
  2. Paste a free Groq or Gemini API key.
  3. Add the contacts that should get replies and write your intro.
  4. Switch Auto-reply ON. Pause or stop any time.

FAQ

Is Girlfriend Automate free?

Yes. Free and MIT licensed. You only pay carrier SMS charges and any AI usage beyond free limits.

Does it tell the other person an AI is replying?

Yes. Every reply starts with your intro and the app forces the word AI to appear.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

No. Normal SMS only.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 9 (API 28) and newer.

Is my chat private?

No server of ours. Recent messages go only to the AI provider you choose when a reply is generated.
